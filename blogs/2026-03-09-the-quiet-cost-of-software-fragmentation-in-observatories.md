Observatory operations often rely on a collection of independent software tools. Except giant ones, like ESO for which I worked in the past, who have enough resources to create a consistent software suite. Telescope control systems, camera software, scheduling tools, data pipelines, weather monitoring, communication channels, and archiving systems typically evolve separately over time.
