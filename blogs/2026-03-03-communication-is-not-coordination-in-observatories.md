Communication Is Not Coordination Many astronomical observatories (both pros and amateurs) coordinate their nightly operations through tools like Slack, Discord, or long email threads. These tools are convenient and familiar, especially for distributed teams. They lower the barrier to communication and make it easy to keep everyone loosely aligned.
